## What is Bama Rush?

Sorority recruitment at the University of Alabama runs for five days every August. Thousands of potential new members (PNMs) cycle through sorority houses in coordinated rounds: Open House, Philanthropy, Sisterhood, Preference Night, and Bid Day.

What makes Alabama's rush unique is scale. UA has one of the largest Greek systems in the country, and the process is intense, ritualized, and — critically — extremely visible on social media.

## What is RushTok?

In 2021, Bama Rush exploded on TikTok under #BamaRush. Women documented their outfits, prep routines, and results in real time. Videos racked up millions of views. The content had a specific structure: OOTD (outfit of the day) breakdowns, with brands named, prices listed, and haul-style reviews of rush prep.

By 2022 and beyond, RushTok had become a recurring annual event — predictable enough that brands started planning around it.

## The brand economy

Rush week functions as a concentrated, high-intent consumer moment. Participants are:

- Buying 5-7 full outfits in a short window
- Spending on pre-rush services (spray tans, lash extensions, blowouts, manicures)
- Purchasing bid-day supplies and sorority-branded merchandise
- Documenting everything on social media

That combination — high spend, high visibility, high documentation — made rush week a target for brands at every price tier.

**Luxury brands** (Van Cleef, Cartier, David Yurman) benefit from organic OOTD placement. A bracelet worn in a viral video reaches millions without a paid deal.

**Mid-market brands** (Lilly Pulitzer, Kendra Scott, Free People) see concentrated sales spikes and use rush-adjacent influencer partnerships.

**Fast fashion brands** (Shein, Nasty Gal) appear as budget alternatives in OOTD cost breakdowns that go viral precisely because of the price contrast.

**Local Tuscaloosa businesses** (spray tan studios, blowout bars, boutiques) report 400% or more sales increases during rush week. The local economy around UA has built entire business models around the annual cycle.

**National service brands** (European Wax Center, Rent the Runway) have sponsored sorority chapters directly.

## How to use this dataset

**Journalists and researchers:** The `rush-brands-public-companies.csv` subset shows which publicly traded companies have material exposure to this consumer moment. Cross-reference with Q3 earnings calls for companies where rush week falls in their fiscal quarter.

**Brand analysts:** The `rush-brands-luxury.csv` subset documents which luxury houses benefit from organic placement vs. paid sponsorship. The `evidence_type` column distinguishes between the two.

**Local economy researchers:** The `rush-brands-tuscaloosa-local.csv` subset documents the hyper-local service economy that has developed around a single university event.

**Media and documentary use:** The full dataset spans 15 categories and 160 sources. The `frequency_signal` column (1-5) indicates how prominently a brand appears across content.

## Methodology

Brands were identified through:

- TikTok OOTD content tagged with #BamaRush, #SororityRush, and related tags
- Published OOTD cost breakdowns (HerCampus, Fast Company, Snobette, and others)
- Local news coverage of Tuscaloosa businesses during rush season
- Sponsored activation announcements and brand press releases
- YouTube rush week documentary content

All sources are listed in `source-ledger/sources.md`.

## Notes on confidence levels

- **High**: Brand confirmed in multiple sources or in direct, named OOTD content
- **Medium**: Brand referenced in one source or inferred from category evidence
- **Low**: Brand appears adjacent to rush content but connection is indirect
